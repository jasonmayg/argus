      Global Data & News collecting

      Available Sources
      federal_reserve gcs gdelt usgs noaa_nws nasa_eonet reliefweb world_bank bank_of_canada fiscal_data sec_edgar fred <custom URL>
      What It Collects

      Multi-source gatherer. Crawls Fed Reserve via Playwright (JavaScript rendering), pulls live events from Guardian POC API adapters (GDELT, USGS, NOAA, NASA EONET, ReliefWeb, World Bank, BoC, FRED, SEC EDGAR, Treasury Auctions), or loads a Guardian alerts snapshot from Google Cloud Storage. Any https:// URL is also accepted as a custom crawl target. All results are normalized to {date, title, link, source, summary}.

      Outputs
      Merged news array Per-source stats Total item count

      Risk Levels & Scoring

      Scores each item 0–100. Critical (80–100) → immediate escalation. High (60–79) → deep analysis. Medium (40–59) → monitor. Low (1–39) → routine.

      Outputs
      Risk score (0–100) Risk level label Category path Matched keywords Distribution stats
